Bio-Hybrid Dental Shield

International Consortium for Electrogenetic Cellular Therapy & Epigenetic Life Extension

Founder: Assoc. Prof. Vyacheslav Kuznetsov, ADNSU

The Tech Stack (Architecture)

1. Intelligent Aligner

A non-invasive, wearable dental mouthguard that acts as a protected outer bioreactor shell, isolating sensitive bio-nanotechnology from oral cavity mechanical strains.

2. DNA-Origami Biochip

A nanoelectronic logic controller arrayed on a programmable macromolecular DNA substrate. It processes salivary biomarkers and translates digital code into electrochemical ionic signals.

3. Stem Cell Incubator

Encapsulated autologous mesenchymal stem cells (MSCs) dynamically triggered by the biochip to produce target therapeutic proteins and youth-inducing exosomes directly into the blood via the buccal mucosa.

Verified Scientific Priority

The core technological framework and claims are officially published and peer-discoverable in the global scientific infrastructure.

Official Identifier: DOI: 10.13140/RG.2.2.30343.15520

Consortium Target Markets

Automated Medicine

On-demand targeted treatment for chronic conditions, autoimmune diseases, and localized oncological suppression bypassing hepatic first-pass metabolism.

Radical Longevity

Systemic age-reversal and senolytic clearance programmed via external epigenetic regulation loops controlled by wearable devices.

Deep Space Exploration

Real-time automated DNA repair matrix and radiation protection system for long-duration human space missions and planetary colonization.
